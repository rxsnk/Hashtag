#Hashtag

#Hashtag is a modern social media platform concept inspired by the most widely used features of Instagram, X (formerly Twitter), 
Facebook, and Messenger. It aims to bring the strengths of these platforms together into one interactive and personalized social 
networking experience.

Unlike traditional social media platforms where users primarily interact through followers, friends, or pages, 
#Hashtag places hashtags at the center of the user experience**. Every post can be associated with one or more hashtags, 
allowing users to discover content, connect with people who share similar interests, and personalize the type of content they encounter.


Concept
The main idea behind #Hashtag** is simple: what you choose to follow defines what you see and who you connect with.
Users can select hashtags based on their interests, hobbies, preferences, communities, or current trends. 
These hashtags can then influence the content displayed on their feed, making the platform more relevant to each individual user.
